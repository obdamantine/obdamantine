Teaching Evaluations

Ashoka University : Academic Year 2024-2025

The Teaching Fellow (TF) Evaluation Survey at Ashoka University back then, was structured to provide the average of scores assigned to certain questions chosen by the Academic Affairs Office to reflect the TF’s teaching. The questions used to evaluate TFs were as follows :

  1. The TF was knowledgeable about the course subject matter.
  2. The TF created student interest and involvement in the discussion sessions.
  3. The TF conducted discussion sessions in an organized and fair manner.
  4. The TF ensured that the discussion sessions complemented the lectures well.
  5. The TF answered student questions professionally and concisely.
The number below Sec I and Sec II is the median rating response in the corresponding section (out of 5.0) to the above indicated average score. The number below Overall is the arithmetic mean (out of 5.0) of all the feedbacks recieved.
